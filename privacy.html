Privacy policy

Last updated November 2026.

Draft. This text is a draft prepared for review and will be finalised before we start taking orders.

1. Who is responsible

Deimos Labs Ltd is the data controller for personal data collected through this website and in the course of orders. Contact: info@deimos-labs.co.uk.

2. What this website collects

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. The basket is stored only in your own browser and is never sent to us. When you prepare an order or a message, the site builds an email on your device; nothing is sent to us until you choose to send it.

3. Data we receive

When you contact us or place an order we receive what you send: typically your name, work email address, institution or company, delivery and billing addresses, purchase order number and the contents of your message. Our email is hosted by a third-party provider. Payment is by bank transfer; we do not collect card details.

4. Why we use it

We use this data to answer your enquiry, to check that products are being supplied for research use, to fulfil and invoice orders, to keep the records that company, tax and product-safety law require, and to deal with any problem with an order. Our lawful bases are performance of a contract, compliance with legal obligations, and our legitimate interest in running the business and preventing misuse of our products. We do not send marketing unless you ask for it, and you can opt out at any time.

5. Who we share it with

Couriers (to deliver your order), our accountants and IT and email providers (to run the business), and public authorities where the law requires. We do not sell personal data.

6. How long we keep it

Order and invoice records are kept for 6 years after the end of the financial year in which the order was made, as UK tax law requires. Enquiries that do not lead to an order are deleted within 12 months.

7. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, object to or restrict its use, and ask for it in a portable form. Email info@deimos-labs.co.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

8. Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page.
