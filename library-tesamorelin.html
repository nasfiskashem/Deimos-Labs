2.Why researchers choose it

Native GHRH is inactivated within minutes when dipeptidyl peptidase IV removes its first two residues. Tesamorelin keeps the complete 44-residue human sequence and caps the N-terminus with a trans-3-hexenoyl group, which protects the Tyr1–Ala2 bond without altering the residues the receptor recognises.

Most stabilised GHRH analogues are truncated to residues 1–29. Tesamorelin is the tool to use when the C-terminal region, residues 30–44, matters to the question, or when a full-length comparator for the short analogues is needed.

3.What the research has found

In non-clinical pharmacology studies the analogue (then TH9507) retained the receptor potency of native GHRH, resisted degradation in plasma and produced sustained growth hormone release in animals, with no activity at receptors outside the GHRH system. [1]

The plasma degradation pathway it is designed to resist was mapped in detail: dipeptidyl peptidase IV cleavage after Ala2 is the dominant route, with trypsin-like cleavage at the basic residues contributing, which is why N-terminal protection alone extends the half-life substantially. [2]

The receptor it acts on, the pituitary GHRH receptor, is a class B G-protein-coupled receptor that signals through adenylyl cyclase, and structure–activity work on the 1–29 fragment defined which residues are essential, context that applies directly to the full-length peptide. [3]

4.Applications at the bench

Growth hormone release from pituitary cells in static culture and perifusion; GHRH receptor binding and cAMP assays; rodent studies of the growth hormone–IGF-1 axis; peptidase stability assays; and side-by-side comparison with native GHRH(1–44) and modified GRF(1–29).

5.Handling notes

Store the lyophilised powder at −20 °C, desiccated. It dissolves in water or dilute acetic acid; a 1 mg/mL stock is typical. The sequence contains a methionine at position 27, so use degassed solvent, aliquot and avoid repeated freeze–thaw cycles to limit oxidation. Two tyrosines and no tryptophan give ε ≈ 2,980 M⁻¹ cm⁻¹ at 280 nm; quantify by HPLC where precision matters.
