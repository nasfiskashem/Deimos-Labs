How every batch is made

From resin to vial

Six stages, in order. A batch that fails a check at any stage does not go on sale.

  1. 01

    Synthesis

    Solid-phase peptide synthesis, residue by residue, from high-grade protected amino acids.

  2. 02

    Purification

    Preparative reverse-phase HPLC separates the target peptide from truncated and deleted sequences.

  3. 03

    Purity check

    Analytical HPLC confirms the batch meets the 98% specification before it moves on.

  4. 04

    Identity check

    Mass spectrometry confirms the molecular weight matches the expected sequence.

  5. 05

    Lyophilisation

    The peptide is freeze-dried to a stable powder and sealed in vials.

  6. 06

    Dispatch

    Each order ships from the UK with the certificate for its exact batch.
