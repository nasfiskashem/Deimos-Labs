Description

Tesamorelin is used as a stabilised full-length GHRH agonist in pituitary cell culture, GHRH receptor signalling assays and rodent studies of growth hormone and IGF-1 regulation, where it is compared with native GHRH(1–44) and the shorter GRF(1–29) analogues.
