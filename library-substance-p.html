Why researchers choose it

Substance P (RPKPQQFFGLM-NH2) is the preferred natural agonist of the NK1 receptor and one of the most thoroughly characterised neuropeptides in existence. It gives robust, reproducible responses in receptor, tissue and behavioural assays, and its biology is relevant to pain, inflammation, mood, emesis and gut function at once.

Our product carries the C-terminal amide that native substance P requires for full receptor activity.

What the research has found

Substance P is a key transmitter of pain signals from sensory neurones and a driver of neurogenic inflammation, triggering vasodilation, plasma extravasation and mast cell degranulation. Research on its receptor led directly to the NK1 antagonist aprepitant, now a standard anti-emetic.

Beyond pain, published work links substance P signalling to wound healing, immune cell trafficking, gut motility, stress responses and the growth and survival of a range of cell types, which keeps it at the centre of neuro-immune research.

Applications at the bench

NK1 receptor binding, internalisation and signalling assays; pain and neurogenic inflammation models; mast cell activation; gut motility preparations; emesis models; and as a reference ligand alongside labelled analogues in receptor localisation studies.

Handling notes

Store at −20 °C, desiccated. Soluble in water and dilute acetic acid. The methionine residue can oxidise, so limit exposure to air in solution and use degassed solvents for long-term stocks. Quantify at 257 nm or by HPLC at 214 nm.
