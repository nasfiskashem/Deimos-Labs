About us

A UK peptide supplier built around documentation.

Deimos Labs supplies synthetic peptides to research groups in universities, hospitals and industry. Researchers kept telling us the same thing: it is hard to find a supplier that sends clear, complete data with every vial. So that is what we focus on.

Each batch is tested by HPLC for purity and by mass spectrometry for identity, and the results travel with the order. If a result does not meet the specification, the batch does not ship.

Who we supply

We supply qualified researchers and organisations for laboratory use. We may ask for institutional details before fulfilling an order, and we do not sell to individuals for personal use.

Get in touch

Email info@deimos-labs.co.uk or use the contact form. We usually reply within one working day.
