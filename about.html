About us

A UK peptide supplier built around documentation.

Every vial we send is backed by its own HPLC and mass spectrometry data. That is the whole idea.

Why Deimos

Deimos is the smaller and more distant of the two moons of Mars. It is not dramatic. It keeps an almost perfectly circular orbit, 23,463 km out, every 30.3 hours, and it has done so for as long as anyone has been able to measure. We chose the name because that is what a research supplier should be: consistent, predictable and exactly where you expect it to be, batch after batch.

What we do

Deimos Labs supplies synthetic peptides to research groups in universities, hospitals and industry. Each peptide is made by solid-phase synthesis, purified by preparative HPLC, checked for purity by analytical HPLC and for identity by mass spectrometry, then freeze-dried and sealed. The results for your exact batch travel with the order.

If a batch does not meet its specification, it does not ship. If a sequence we don't list would help your work, we can make it to your specification, with the same testing.

Who we supply

We supply qualified researchers and organisations for laboratory use. We may ask for institutional details before fulfilling an order, and we do not sell to individuals for personal use.
