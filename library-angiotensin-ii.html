2.Why researchers choose it

Angiotensin II (DRVYIHPF) is the principal effector of the renin–angiotensin system and the reference ligand for AT1 and AT2 receptors. Because the human, rat and bovine sequences are identical, one product serves almost every mammalian model, and results translate cleanly across species.

It produces strong, reproducible responses at nanomolar concentrations in both cell and tissue preparations, and its signalling pathways are among the best characterised of any peptide hormone.

3.What the research has found

Decades of work have mapped angiotensin II's control of vascular tone, aldosterone release, sodium handling and thirst, and its roles in vascular remodelling, cardiac hypertrophy, fibrosis and inflammation. Much of this knowledge underpins ACE inhibitors and AT1 blockers, two of the most widely used drug classes in the world. [1]

Chronic infusion in rodents remains the standard way to produce hypertension, cardiac hypertrophy, aortic aneurysm and kidney injury for drug testing, and is the model behind a large share of published cardiovascular pharmacology. [2]

4.Applications at the bench

AT1 and AT2 receptor binding, calcium-flux and reporter assays; contraction of isolated vessel rings; chronic infusion models of hypertension, hypertrophy and fibrosis; vascular smooth muscle and cardiomyocyte signalling; and as a substrate and standard in angiotensinase and mass-spectrometry work.

5.Handling notes

Store at −20 °C, desiccated. Soluble in water to at least 1 mg/mL; acidify slightly if cloudy. The tyrosine residue allows quantification at 280 nm. Aliquot and freeze; include protease inhibitors in serum-containing systems.
