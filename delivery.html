Delivery and returns

Dispatch

Orders are dispatched from the UK once payment or a valid purchase order has been received. We aim to dispatch within two working days.

Delivery

All orders are sent by tracked delivery, and you'll receive the tracking number by email when your order ships. Delivery charges are shown on your invoice.

Packaging

Peptides ship as lyophilised powder in sealed vials, which are stable at ambient temperature in transit. On arrival, store them at −20 °C.

Damaged or incorrect orders

If anything arrives damaged, incorrect or missing, email info@deimos-labs.co.uk within 7 days with your order details and a photo where possible. We'll replace the item or refund it.

Returns

Because peptides are sensitive to storage conditions, we can't accept returns of opened vials. Unopened, sealed items can be returned within 14 days of delivery. Contact us first and we'll arrange it.

Who we supply

We supply businesses, universities, hospitals and research organisations only, and we do not sell to individuals for personal use. Orders need the organisation's name and a work email address.
