What it is

BPC-157 (Body Protection Compound 157) is a synthetic pentadecapeptide with the sequence Gly-Glu-Pro-Pro-Pro-Gly-Lys-Pro-Ala-Asp-Asp-Ala-Gly-Leu-Val. It is a partial sequence of a larger protein isolated from human gastric juice by a group at the University of Zagreb, who have published most of the literature on it.

It is supplied as a lyophilised powder, usually as the free acid or an acetate salt. It is stable in water for several days at room temperature and resists degradation by gastric acid, which is unusual for a peptide of this length.

Where it is used in research

Most published studies use rat models of tendon, ligament, muscle and bone injury, gut lesions and vascular occlusion, with the peptide given by injection or in drinking water. A smaller body of work uses cell culture, particularly fibroblast and endothelial cell lines, to look at migration and growth-factor signalling.

Reported areas of interest include the nitric oxide system, VEGF and early growth response gene expression, and interactions with the dopamine and serotonin systems in behavioural models. Nearly all of the data comes from a single research group, so independent replication is a live question in the field.

Handling notes

Store the lyophilised powder at −20 °C, desiccated. Reconstitute in sterile water or saline; a stock of 1 mg/mL is typical. Aliquot after reconstitution and avoid repeated freeze–thaw cycles.

BPC-157 has no aromatic residues, so it absorbs weakly at 280 nm. Quantify by weight or by HPLC at 214 nm rather than by UV absorbance at 280.

Regulatory status

BPC-157 is not an approved medicine anywhere in the world and is listed by the World Anti-Doping Agency as a prohibited substance. Deimos Labs supplies it for laboratory research only.
