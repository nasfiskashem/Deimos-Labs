Payment methods

When you send an order request from your basket, we confirm stock and email you an invoice. You can pay it in either of these ways.

Bank transfer

Pay by UK bank transfer using the details on your invoice. Use your invoice number as the payment reference so we can match it quickly. Your order is dispatched once the payment arrives.

Purchase order

Universities, hospitals and companies can order against a purchase order. Add your PO number at checkout or send the PO to info@deimos-labs.co.uk. Invoices on purchase orders are payable within 30 days.

Questions

If you need a pro-forma invoice, supplier forms or our bank details for your finance team, contact us.
