The basics

What does Deimos Labs sell?

Synthetic peptides for laboratory research, supplied as lyophilised powder in sealed vials. We also synthesise custom sequences on request.

Who can buy from you?

We supply researchers, laboratories and organisations for in-vitro and laboratory research. We may ask for institutional details before we fulfil an order, and we do not sell for personal use.

Can I use these products in people or animals?

No. Every product is for laboratory research use only. None is approved for human or veterinary use, and we give no dosing or administration guidance.

Does the calculator tell me how much to use?

No. The reconstitution calculator does arithmetic on values you enter: it turns a vial mass and a diluent volume into a concentration, and an amount into a volume. It has no defaults and does not recommend any amount, concentration, solvent or protocol. We do not provide dosing or administration guidance for any product.

Quality and testing

How do you test each batch?

Each batch is tested by reverse-phase HPLC for purity and by mass spectrometry to confirm identity. Batches that miss the specification are not sold.

Do I get a certificate of analysis?

Yes. A certificate for the exact batch you receive is included with every order. You can also ask us for the current certificate before ordering.

What purity can I expect?

Our standard specification is 98% or higher by HPLC. Higher purity grades are available for custom orders.

Storage and handling

How should I store peptides?

Store lyophilised peptides at −20 °C, sealed and away from moisture and light. Let the vial reach room temperature before opening.

How long do they keep?

Stored correctly as dry powder, most peptides stay stable for well over a year. Once in solution, aliquot and freeze to avoid repeated freeze–thaw cycles.

How do I reconstitute a peptide?

Let the vial reach room temperature before opening, add your chosen solvent slowly down the inside wall, and swirl rather than shake. Our handling guide covers solvent choice by sequence, aliquoting and storage, and the calculator turns your masses and volumes into concentrations.

Ordering and delivery

How do I order?

Add products to your basket and send the order request. We confirm stock and send an invoice with payment details, usually within one working day. Purchase orders are welcome.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer against our invoice. Institutions can pay against a purchase order number.

Where do you deliver?

We ship from the UK with tracked delivery. Orders are dispatched once payment or a valid purchase order is received.

Can I return an order?

Because peptides are sensitive to storage conditions, we cannot accept returns of opened vials. If an order arrives damaged or incorrect, contact us within 7 days and we will replace it.
